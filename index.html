The local shop

Small-business care, one arrangement at a time.

Awe Flowers & Potted Plants was founded by Patricia Sheldon in 2017 and continues to serve the Lake Worth community with a local small-business approach.

The shop’s own story emphasizes quality floral design, personal customer service and thoughtful attention to each order. That matters when the flowers are carrying a message you may not know how to put into words.
